# ART Benchmarks

* Time & Space
  * Deduplicating Words in Moby Dick
  * Deduplicating Words in Complete Works of Shakespeare
  * 1 Million Shuffled Uint32s, Point Operations (Insert, Search, Remove)
  * 1 Million UUIDs, Point Operations (Insert, Search, Remove)
* Time Only
  * Set Operations (Intersection, Difference, Union) on Distinct Words in Moby Dick and Distinct Words in Shakespeare
  * String Prefix Operations (Membership Test, Iteration)
  * Bounded Range Queries of Compound Keys 
    * 2x Uint8, 60K
    * 4x Uint8, 100K, 1M, 10M
    * 8x Uint8
    * 16x Uint8
    * 32x Uint8
    * 2x Uint16
    * 4x Uint16
    * 8x Uint16
    * 16x Uint16
    * 32x Uint16
